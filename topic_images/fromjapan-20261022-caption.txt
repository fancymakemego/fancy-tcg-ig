🍙 From Japan: the 7-Eleven Bento Pikachu

Back in summer 2015, 7-Eleven Japan ran a Pokémon bento campaign. You reserved the set at the store, and it came with a Pikachu-face lunch box, chopsticks, a handkerchief… and this promo card. 🍱

The art by Kanako Eo is pure picnic: Pikachu, Chespin and Sylveon sharing rice balls and bread. And the attack? "Let's Eat Together" – both Active Pokémon heal 30 HP. 😄

It was never in a booster pack, so the only way to get one was to order a bento in Japan.
